# 词笺（CiJi）隐私政策

最近更新：2026-09-25

词笺是一款英语词库应用。我们不要求注册账号，也不用词笺收集可识别你身份的资料来做广告或追踪。

## 存在你设备上的内容

单词、分组、练习记录和偏好设置保存在你的设备上。若你登录了 iCloud，这些内容会通过你的 Apple ID 同步到你自己的 iCloud，供同一账号下的 iPhone 与 Mac 使用。我们无法读取你的 iCloud 词库。

## 联网

查询音标、中文释义和发音时，应用会把你输入的英文发到词典服务，包括有道词典、Free Dictionary API 和 Datamuse。这些请求只用于返回读音和释义。

## 我们不做的事

不出售数据，不接入第三方广告或追踪，不要求额外登录。

## 联系

如有隐私问题，请发邮件至：mokangtong@gmail.com

# CiJi Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

CiJi is an English vocabulary app. It does not ask you to create an account, and it does not use your information for advertising or tracking.

## What stays on your devices

Words, groups, practice records, and preferences are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, this content syncs through your Apple ID to your own iCloud, so the same library can appear on your iPhone and Mac. We cannot read your iCloud library.

## Network access

When you look up a phonetic spelling, a Chinese meaning, or a pronunciation, the app sends the English text you entered to dictionary services, including Youdao Dictionary, the Free Dictionary API, and Datamuse. Those requests are used only to return pronunciation and meanings.

## What we do not do

We do not sell data, show third-party ads, use tracking, or require a separate login.
