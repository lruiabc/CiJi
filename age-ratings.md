# CiJi Age Rating

Choose **4+**. CiJi is a personal vocabulary notebook. It has no chat, ads, violence, or adult content.

Answer **No** or **None** for every item.

Dictionary lookups call Youdao and similar services. That is not an in-app browser, so Unrestricted Web Access is **No**. Words stay on the user’s devices and in their own iCloud. Other people cannot see them, so User-Generated Content is **No**.

Do not turn on **Made for Kids**. That category has stricter rules, and this app uses the network to look up words.
